How It Works

  1. Enter API Key — get yours at triverse.ai and paste it above.
  2. Upload or Describe — provide a reference image or a text prompt of your scene.
  3. Tune Parameters — adjust depth, smoothness, and thickness to match your needs.
  4. Generate — the AI will create a 3D bas-relief model. Generation takes ~30–90 s.
  5. Preview & Download — rotate the 3D model, then download STL/GLB for printing or use.
